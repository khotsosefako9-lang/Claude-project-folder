# Duly Qualified — storylines for the three WhatsApp videos

One storyline is picked for the whole series; each video applies it to its own script
(`SCRIPTS.md`). Format for all: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 35–37 s (the voiceover's length),
cloned voiceover as the clock, burned-in captions (Status is often watched muted).

Brand: navy `#14213D`, ivory `#F2F1EC`, red `#C8372D` rationed to the one loss/alarm
moment and the Q's tail on the end card. DQ monogram lands once, on the end card.

---

## A. The paper trail — recommended (the viewer's own paperwork tells the story)

**Device:** a moment in someone's day. **Form:** object-to-object chain — every shot ends on
the paper or phone that opens the next.

The viewer sees the things a small business owner actually handles — a WhatsApp message, a
tender notice, a COIDA letter, a site hoarding — passed hand to hand until the loss is
obvious, then the same chain run again with Duly Qualified's monthly sample catching it in
time. It feels familiar and a little painful, then relieving.

- **First 2 s:**
  1. *Tender:* a WhatsApp bubble on ivory — "Did you see the guarding tender? 🤦🏾 Closed Friday." — the date strikes through in red.
  2. *Technicality:* a red **DISQUALIFIED** stamp slams onto a bid cover page.
  3. *Cameras:* a "COMING SOON — shopping centre" hoarding; camera icons pop onto every corner.
- **Beats:** hook (the loss) → the owner's world (firm, guards, clients) → the missed item in close-up (closing date / expired letter / someone else's van) → "Duly Qualified" reveal → the monthly sample page fills: tenders + closing dates / checklist + expiry reminders / new sites + contact + first message → end card.
- **Look:** warm ivory paper ground, navy ink type (bold sans, large), real document layouts as flat cards with paper texture; red only for the strike, stamp or expiry date.
- **Sound:** voice leads; a soft, steady minimal bed (≈95 bpm) that drops out on the loss line and returns when Duly Qualified is named; a paper slide on each hand-off, one stamp hit in video 2, pop on the end-card mark.

## B. The words that cost you

**Device:** type carries the argument. **Form:** cut on the voice — colour fields flip on every sentence.

Giant navy and ivory type fills the vertical frame, a few words at a time, timed to the voice:
"TWELVE GUARDS." "GOOD CLIENTS." "CLOSED FRIDAY." The one word that costs the business money
lands in red and holds. Bold, fast, and the strongest of the three with the sound off.

- **First 2 s:**
  1. "CLOSED" in red on navy, then "FRIDAY."
  2. "DISQUALIFIED." in red, letters slamming in.
  3. "SOMEONE ELSE'S" / "CAMERAS." with a camera-shutter cut.
- **Beats:** the cost word → who they are (3 short type beats) → what happened (2–3 beats) → "Duly Qualified finds them" → what you get, one item per beat → URL → end card.
- **Look:** full-bleed navy / ivory fields alternating, one heavy condensed sans in caps, no images; red for exactly one word per video.
- **Sound:** voice plus a tight percussive pulse (≈110 bpm), cuts on its beats; a single low hit under the red word; silence for half a beat before the reveal.

## C. One map of the Bay — the bold one

**Device:** the object is the explanation. **Form:** a flight through one world.

A navy map of Nelson Mandela Bay (from OpenStreetMap). The camera flies over it: the owner's
business is a pin; tenders open and close as pulses across the metro, new buildings rise as
blocks — and each one the owner misses fades to red and goes dark. Then Duly Qualified pins
them in time, and the map fills with ivory points.

- **First 2 s:**
  1. A pulse opens over a government office in the city, a countdown runs "Fri", and it goes dark in red before the camera reaches it.
  2. A bid document on the map flips to "EXPIRED" in red.
  3. A new shopping centre extrudes up from the map, cameras lighting on each corner.
- **Beats:** the missed opportunity on the map → pull back to the owner's pin in the Bay → more pulses opening and closing unseen → "Duly Qualified" reveal → the camera revisits each pulse, now caught, with closing date / expiry / contact → end card.
- **Look:** navy ground, ivory street lines and labels (OSM data, attribution on the end card), red for missed items; one shared camera move per video.
- **Sound:** an airy, wide pad (≈80 bpm) under the voice; soft pings as pulses open; the music lifts when the map fills; a low riser into the end card.
